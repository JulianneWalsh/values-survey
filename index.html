How to use a ternary plot

Each corner of the triangle is one value. The closer your dot is to a corner, the more important that value is. A dot in the middle means all three are equally important. There are no right or wrong answers.

  1. Dot A sits near the top corner: Economic Prosperity is given most importance (70%), Equality and Environmental Protection 15% each.
  2. Dot B sits in the middle: all three values are about equally important (about 33% each).
  3. Dot C sits near the bottom-left corner: Equality is given most importance (80%), the other two 10% each.

You will place one dot for society today, then one dot for society in 2060. Click or tap to place the dot, then click elsewhere or drag to move it. Prefer a keyboard? Use the three sliders under each triangle.

Step 2 of 2

Society in 2060

The scenario. By 2060, as extreme climate change became apparent, with rising sea levels and flooding, private insurers began refusing to insure the worst-affected land. This had a knock-on effect: no insurance meant no mortgages, causing a crash in the property market. Houses in these areas became practically worthless, driving up prices further inland and causing an influx of migrants moving inland. Coastal cities became full of people too poor to leave, minimally governed and severely neglected. The contrast not only in life expectancy but also in available facilities and quality of life grew strikingly obvious, driven by the varying population density.

In the world described above, how much importance do you believe society will place on each of these three values?
